# Details

The small things that make this site feel finished. People notice them without naming them; apply the ones that fit your project.

- **Frosted glass**: 9 surfaces blur what's behind them (backdrop-filter), usually headers, menus and overlays.
- **Smooth scrolling**: Scrolling is smoothed by Lenis; the page glides rather than jumps.
- **Blend modes**: 5 elements blend into what's behind them (mix-blend-mode).
- **Filters**: Images and shapes are treated with blur (79), contrast (8), grayscale (5).
- **3D CSS**: 60 elements use perspective or preserve-3d for depth without WebGL.
- **Grain texture**: A fine noise texture sits over surfaces (an SVG turbulence filter or noise image), so flat colour feels printed.
- **Focus ring**: Keyboard focus shows as rgb(77, 101, 255) solid 0.125rem, offset 0.125rem (:focus-visible).
